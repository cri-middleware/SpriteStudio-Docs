# 🎮 SSPlayerForUnity

Play back SpriteStudio 7 animations in the **Unity Engine**. Playback runs through
`libssruntime` (the shared Rust core) combined with Unity's Job System for high performance.

- **Documentation:** _coming soon_
- **Repository:** <https://github.com/cri-middleware/SSPlayerForUnity>

See [Choose Your Platform](../overview/choose-your-platform.md) to compare players, or
[SDK Concepts](../sdk/concepts.md) for the conversion/playback pipeline shared across all of them.
